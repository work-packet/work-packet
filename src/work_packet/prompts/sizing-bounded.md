## How much to do

Do one step's worth of work, write the two files, and stop. A step's worth
is one item of the plan, carried to a point where it is committed or
otherwise safe to leave: no half-made edits, no uncommitted files.

- Once an item is done, start the next only if this step's context is still
  under about half its limit. Otherwise hand off now: the next step starts
  with a fresh context, and research you start but cannot finish is lost,
  because the next step reads the same files again.
- Hand on decisions and the exact next action, not research notes. "In
  `src/parse.c`, change `read_token` (line 212) to …" is worth more than a
  page on how the file works.
